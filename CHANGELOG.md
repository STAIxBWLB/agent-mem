## 0.20.0

- **Final transcript hooks interleave scan and replay** (task 110, Refs #226). The single worker replays after durable summary publication, on actual spool backpressure, and once before exit, reusing one CaptureStore and one shared 32-file / 256 KiB replay allowance. The fixed 16-record order switch and 60/45 ms split are removed; the live hard deadline, 256 KiB transcript read, cursor commit allowance and bounded parent reap remain. Ordinary PostToolUse boot timing is unchanged, and its worker no longer imports the unused transcript module. Worker-path fixtures isolate a blocking append without importing the unrelated cold store implementation; explicit host-relative bounds, unconditional entry, zero survivors and retained records remain checked; the hosted budget harness provides native 140/300 ms proof.
- **A reader no longer refuses at the moment a public prune finishes** (#225). When the cooperating writer unlinked its intent and lock between a reader's open and fstat, the reader saw a link count of 0 and refused with `public prune view unavailable`, although its wait bound had time left. That disappearance is now treated like an absent file: the bytes read through the old descriptor are discarded and the path is resolved again within the same deadline. Any other link count, a foreign mode or a symlink still refuses, and the 3 s wait bound is unchanged.
- **Hosted R2 RSS gate uses p95 over a fixed count** (#228, owner decision 2026-11-02). The actual-hook budget scripts gate RSS on the nearest-rank p95 of each group's fixed samples (transcript groups: each logical run's peak, 40 per group) instead of the single maximum. The limit stays 100 MB everywhere except one named exception: hosted GitHub Linux transcript StopBurst gates at p95 < 110 MB until the unexplained shift of about 7 MB from 9986fe6 is understood. Every sample is still logged, unknown telemetry fails closed, nothing is retried, and each run logs a runner fingerprint. Measurement scripts only; no product code changes.
- **R2 baseline reset** (#228, owner decision 2026-11-02). The hook budgets now gate on a 150 MB ceiling for every invocation, a regression of more than 10% over a committed per-runner, per-group p95 baseline (`docs/bench/r2-baseline.json`, seeded from retained main and dgx02 runs), and a 300 ms per-invocation maximum on the handler's own metric wall (the outer CLI wall is reported only) that a group retry cannot clear. The hosted-linux StopBurst 110 MB override is removed. The transcript harness reports the 300 ms maximum until the #226 scan redesign.
- **Transcript hooks always account for their stop** (#221). A Claude Stop or SessionEnd hook with a transcript that runs out of time before or around its worker now records `transcriptStopReason: "deadline"`, but only when the remaining time measured at that exit is below the worker's start threshold; a hook that staging declines (policy, format floor, invalid input) still records no reason. A transcript scan that throws records the new reason `unavailable`, which no budget clause accepts as progress. Production deadlines and limits are unchanged.
- **Transcript stall check is advisory on hosted macOS only** (#226, owner decision 2026-11-02). In `measure-transcript-budget.ts` the "stalled after three reason-only invocations" clause logs the full sample and a warning instead of failing, only with `CI=true`, `GITHUB_ACTIONS=true` and `RUNNER_OS=macOS`. Every other progress, no-loss, RSS and wall clause, and this clause on dgx02, local runs and hosted ubuntu, stay hard failures. The #226 redesign (below) did not clear it on the 3-vCPU hosted macOS runner, so it stays until the early-receipt follow-up after 0.20.0.
- **Transcript hooks cannot be held past their deadline** (#226, task 107). A Claude Stop or SessionEnd hook now starts its one capture worker at hook start, so the transcript scan runs while native metadata resolves, and the worker drains once the native receipt arrives. At the deadline the hook kills the worker and waits at most 10 ms for it, so a worker stuck finishing a disk write can no longer hold the hook past its 140 ms handler (hosted ubuntu had reached 330 ms). A kill skips that call's drain; the staged summaries stay durable and pending for the next hook, and the metric records `drainSkipped` (not a capture drop) and `abandonedChildren`. When 16 or more of a folder's records are already pending, the worker saves them before it scans, so a slow machine cannot fill the spool and stall capture; the metric records when the save started (`drainStartMs`) and how many records it saved (`drained`). The transcript budget enforces the 300 ms handler maximum, and its stall check stays advisory on hosted macOS only (above) and strict everywhere else. R1 allows a killed worker at most 2 s to leave the kernel after the hook exits. A budget stall over 300 ms, or without a known handler wall, can no longer be cleared by the slow-runner retry.
- Hook-spool pressure protection (#217, PR1): directory identity uses inode and birth time across reboots; per-cwd 64/8 active/reserve shares, one-hour retirement of positively missing cwds, and doctor fill/classification reporting make orphaned backlogs bounded and visible. Accepted summaries survive same-cwd pressure; new summaries backpressure without advancing the transcript cursor. Native retirement uses `scope_capacity`; proven foreign all-summary retirement uses `summary_capacity`, each counted before removal. SessionStart leaves maintenance cursors untouched when its reserve is not pressured.
- **Off-hook spool replay** (#217 PR2). `amem spool replay --json [--source <dir>]` previews, read-only and without record bodies, which orphaned or parked hook records can be replayed and why the rest cannot; `amem spool replay --apply --plan <file>` applies that saved preview. Each record's project comes from its own cwd, policy applies per record, a record whose identity this boot cannot prove (for example one written before a reboot) is listed as a cwd -> project mapping that `--confirm-unverifiable` replays only after the person checks it, unknown schemas stay pending, the saved plan resumes its own claims after a crash or a run that stopped partway (records it already finished are reported `absent`), a record is removed only after its capture is durable, and running out of open files fails the preview loudly instead of returning a short one.
- **Compatibility with N-1 (0.19.0):** hook spool: upgrade every amem that shares the memory home (#217). 0.19.0 cannot drain the new reboot-stable-scope records and can retire them after seven days; they need a current reader. The current reader accepts legacy scopes from the current boot, but pre-upgrade records bound to a previous boot's device stay pending until `amem spool replay` (#217 PR2) or the TTL. A replay claim uses the existing `.draining-` suffix form, which 0.19.0 already treats as a claimed record. No format floor is raised automatically.
